Privacy Policy — Caffeine Vessel

Last updated: September 1, 2026

In plain language: Caffeine Vessel stores everything you enter directly on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. If you never turn on the optional self-hosted sync feature, your data never leaves your device.

What information the app collects

To estimate how caffeine affects you, the app asks you to answer a short set of questions when you first set it up, and stores what you log afterward:

  • Setup answers: your age range, weight, usual bedtime, and a few health-related questions (whether you have trouble sleeping, smoke, drink alcohol heavily, are a heavy caffeine drinker, have liver disease, or take certain medications that affect how caffeine is processed).
  • Caffeine log entries: the drinks you log, their caffeine content, and the time you logged them.

This information is used only to calculate an estimate of the caffeine level currently active in your body and a forecast for your bedtime. It is not used for any other purpose.

Where this information is stored

By default, all of the information above is stored locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage. The app does not require an account, does not create a profile on any server, and does not transmit this information anywhere.

Optional self-hosted sync

Caffeine Vessel's underlying software supports an optional mode where a technically inclined user can connect the app to a server they set up and control themselves (for example, on their own home network), so their data can sync across their own devices. This is not enabled in the version of the app distributed on the Play Store, and turning it on requires manual technical configuration outside the app itself. If you never do this, it does not apply to you. If a user does configure this, their data is sent only to the server they personally control — never to the app's developer or any third party.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash reporting or diagnostic data collection.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No sale or sharing of your information with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries at any time from within the app. Settings also includes an option to erase all logged data. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your device.

Health information disclaimer

Caffeine Vessel provides estimates for personal tracking and general wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. See the in-app disclaimer shown during setup for more detail.

Children

Caffeine Vessel is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
